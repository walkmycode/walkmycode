Move: handing over the ticket

A String is a small ticket on the stack that points at text on the heap.

let s2 = s1; copies the ticket, not the text. Now only s2 is valid.

Two valid tickets would mean two frees of one coat: a double free.

💡 AnalogyYou give your cloakroom ticket to a friend. The coat never moves; only the ticket changes hands. Now you have no ticket, so you cannot collect the coat.

Why can't I use s1 after the move?
ProfessorCopy · Save to notebook · Read aloud

Because s1 gave its ticket away. Rust marks it as empty so nobody frees the same text twice.

💡 Your friend now holds the cloakroom ticket. You can still stand at the desk, but you have nothing to hand over.
