How it feels

  • Just talk. Hands-free by default, and you can cut in at any time: he stops, listens, then answers in 1–3 sentences and hands the turn back with a question.
  • He knows where you are. The board on screen, what you've seen, your notes. When the board changes, he knows.
  • He drives the lesson while you talk. "Let's see the next board", "Open the example", "Quick quiz?", "Explain it back to me".
  • He sounds human. A quiet "mm-hmm" while you speak (with headphones), "take your time" if you go quiet, and he slows down when you sound stuck. Voice style from Settings (Lively by default).
  • You can see who has the floor. His ring: gold while he speaks, green and swelling with your voice while you speak, dashed while he thinks, pink when he checks on you.
  • Nothing is lost. Spoken turns land in the conversation, marked 🎙, kept with the lesson.
