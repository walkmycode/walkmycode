When it showsOnce per launch, a few seconds after the app opens, only in installed builds. "Skip this version" stays quiet until a newer one comes out. "Later" or Esc closes the modal and leaves the yellow Update chip in the title bar to reopen it.
What's newRead from the release's own notes (latest.json): Features become New, Bug Fixes become Fixed, Performance becomes Better, and the scope becomes the area (Learn, Explore…). Links to the private repo and commit hashes are removed. The newest five items show, then "All release notes".
InstallingA real progress bar from the updater's byte counts, with MB and speed. The app's server stops just before the installer runs, then WalkMyCode reopens by itself. If anything fails, nothing has changed: Try again, or the download page.
