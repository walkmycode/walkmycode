WalkMyCode desktop: the title bar

No window frame. WalkMyCode's own top bar is the title bar: drag it from any empty space, double-click it to maximise or restore, and use the three buttons at its right end. Every other control in the bar works exactly as in the browser, and in a browser the bar is unchanged (the second window below).

Drags the windowThe bar's background, the brand, the title and its detail. Double-click any of them: maximise or restore. Dragging a maximised window down restores it; dragging to a screen edge snaps (Windows Snap, and the Linux desktop's own tiling).
Never dragsButtons, links, inputs and menus: Explore / Learn, the project chip, the room's actions, the AI pill, Settings and the window buttons.
Window buttons46 × 32 px, the bar's full height, so the top-right pixel of a maximised window still closes it. Close turns red on hover. Maximise shows "restore" (two squares) while maximised. Dimmed while the window is in the background.
EdgesThe window resizes from all four edges and corners. It never gets smaller than 960 × 600, and opens where and how big it was last time.
